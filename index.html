01 — About

Design first. Then I ship it.

I'm Adnan A. Laskar — a graphic designer and frontend developer from South Delhi. I'm finishing my STEM stream at Dr. B.R. Ambedkar School of Specialised Excellence, Kalkaji, and I spend most of my hours somewhere between a design file and a code editor.

Design is where I start: typography, layout rhythm, colour grading and the small details that make something feel intentional. Development is how I finish it — hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript that stays fast and readable.

I've run 6 hackathons, including a Top 10 finish at Hackside Down 2026 with OpenFlash. Off-screen I play piano and keyboard, and I keep a serious interest in social psychology and human activism.
